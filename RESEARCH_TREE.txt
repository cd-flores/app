NEBULA DOMINION - RESEARCH TREE
===============================

How research works
------------------
- Build a Research Hub with a Harvester (select it, press 2, click open space; 400 Energy, 400 Metal). Only one is allowed.
- Select the Hub (press B) and click a tech to research it. One tech at a time.
- The cost is paid up front. Cancelling refunds it in full.
- If the Hub is destroyed, the research in progress is lost.
- "Tier" is the Hub tier a tech needs. The Hub starts at tier 1; raise it with the Hub Expansion techs.

Rare items (extracted by Research Outposts; build a Surveyor at the Hub)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Pyro    = Pyrocrystal      from Volcanic planets       (1 every 45s)
  Cryo    = Cryo Core        from Ice planets            (1 every 45s)
  Plasma  = Plasma Essence   from Gas giants             (1 every 45s)
  Void    = Void Shard       from Crystal worlds         (1 every 45s)
  Relic   = Relic Fragment   from Ancient Ruins          (1 every 90s; guarded, conquer first)

Cost format: Energy / Metal / Rare items (energy includes the +50 surcharge)     Time in seconds

-----------------------------

SUGGESTED PATH TO THE LEVIATHAN
-------------------------------
1. Build the Hub, then 2-3 Surveyors on different barren planet types.
2. Hub Expansion II  (needs 1 of each: Pyro, Cryo, Plasma, Void)
3. Conquer an Ancient Ruins planet and put an outpost on it for Relics.
4. Hub Expansion III (2 Relic)
5. Leviathan Project (6 Void, 3 Relic)
6. Build the Leviathan (3 Pyro, 5 Void, 3 Relic + 20 housing)
7. Optional: Nova Cannon, then Capital Fleet Command


SCARCE FINDS AND LATE TECHS
---------------------------
Scarce finds turn up at low odds (they appear in the top bar once you have found one):
  Dark     = Dark Matter      harvesters unloading asteroid metal (2%/load), Asteroid Miners (4%/min)
  Star     = Star Essence     Solar Collectors (4%/min), harvesters unloading starlight (1%/load)
  Neut     = Neutronium       salvaging a wreck to the end (8%); pirate stronghold wrecks always hold 2
  Exotic   = Exotic Matter    Ruins outposts (12% per relic), destroyed pirate Dreadnoughts (25%)

Neutronium Plating        Tier 3   1550E / 2000M / 4 Neut              100s
    Requires: Reinforced Hulls II
    -> +20% more hull HP (all ships)
Hyperdrive Lattice        Tier 3   1250E / 1000M / 2 Dark, 2 Star, 1 Exotic   90s
    Requires: Ion Engines
    -> +10% ship speed; jump gates recharge instantly
Dark Matter Reactors      Tier 3   1550E / 1500M / 4 Dark              100s
    Requires: Rapid Assembly
    -> Another +30% build speed everywhere

Lab upgrades: each Research Hub tier (Hub Expansion II / III) also researches 25% faster.
